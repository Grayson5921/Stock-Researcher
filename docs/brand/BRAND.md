## 1. The name

**Nine Critics** (long form: **Nine Critics Research**)

Why it works:
- **It IS the product.** Every stock idea faces a panel of nine skeptical AI
  critics and needs 8 of 9 to survive. The name states the mechanism — no
  explanation needed.
- **It sells the differentiator.** Every stock-tips site promises winners.
  Nine Critics promises *scrutiny* — the anti-hype position, which is both the
  honest framing and the legally safer one (research tool, not advice).
- **The story writes itself.** "Your idea versus nine critics." "Survived
  8 of 9." "One dissenter allowed." Marketing copy falls out of the mechanics.
- Short, spellable, pronounceable, no finance clichés (avoids "capital,"
  "wealth," "advisor" — words that invite investment-adviser optics).

Alternates (if clearance fails): **Skeptiq** (skeptic + IQ, brandable),
**The Second Opinion**, **Redline Research**. Avoid "Gauntlet" — an established
crypto risk-modeling firm already uses it in finance.

### Name clearance (do before buying domains/filing anything)
1. Search the USPTO trademark database (tmsearch.uspto.gov) for "Nine Critics"
   in classes 36 (financial) and 42 (software/SaaS).
2. Domain check: `ninecritics.com` first choice; `ninecritics.ai`,
   `ninecritics.co`, `getninecritics.com` as fallbacks.
3. Social handles: `@ninecritics` on X, Reddit, YouTube.
4. State business registration search (for your LLC/DBA filing).

**The mark:** a 3×3 panel of nine tiles — eight solid (approvals), one outlined

Usage rules: don't rotate, recolor, or add effects; keep clear space equal to
one tile around the mark; on photos, use the dark variant on a scrim. The
wordmark uses the system font stack — convert text to outlines before print use.

## 4. Positioning

**One-liner:** Nine skeptical AI critics stress-test a stock idea — and tell
you what survives.

**Positioning statement:** For self-directed retail investors who are drowning
in hype, Nine Critics is an automated research tool that runs low/mid-cap stock
ideas through a nine-critic gauntlet — fundamentals, governance, valuation,
risk, solvency, timing, and more — with live web research, adversarial debate,
news and bull/bear simulation gates. Unlike stock-tip newsletters, it shows the
full argument, including every rejection, and a "nothing passed" result is an
honest, complete deliverable.

**Audience:** DIY investors (r/stocks, FinTwit readers) who already research
their own picks; small-cap hunters; people burned by pump content who want a
devil's advocate, not a guru.

**Differentiators, in order:**
1. **Adversarial by design** — 9 critics, 90 criteria, 8-of-9 to pass, and the
   researcher must argue back or concede. You watch the debate live.
2. **Radical transparency** — the report includes every verdict, every
   rebuttal, and every rejected stock with reasons.
3. **Grounded** — live prices, SEC EDGAR filings, and news; claims are
   fact-checked before the panel ever sees them.
4. **Honest failure mode** — "nothing passed this week" is a real answer, and
   you still get the full documentation of why.

**What we never say (compliance guardrails for ALL copy):**
- Never promise or imply returns ("beat the market," "winners," "can't-miss").
- Never call outputs "advice," "recommendations to buy," or "picks" — use
  *research*, *analysis*, *verdicts*, *candidates that survived*.
- Every page, ad, and report carries the disclaimer: automated research tool,
  not investment advice, may be wrong, verify independently, you are solely
  responsible for trades.
- No testimonials about investment performance.

## 7. Launch channels (first 90 days, $0 ad budget)

1. **Build in public** on X + r/SideProject: post real (anonymized) debate
   screenshots — the critic verdicts landing live are the demo.
2. **Content that shows the mechanism:** "We ran [well-known small cap] through
   nine critics — here's who dissented and why" as blog/X threads. Rejections
   are more shareable than approvals and safer than implied picks.
3. **Product Hunt / Hacker News (Show HN)** once ~10 real runs have validated
   cost + stability.
4. **Reddit** (r/stocks, r/pennystocks adjacent — read each sub's self-promo
   rules; lead with the free "nothing passed" transparency angle, never returns).
5. **SEO:** one page per critic persona ("What a governance hawk checks before
   buying a small cap") — evergreen, educational, compliance-safe.

Metrics that matter early: visitor → signup rate, signup → first paid run,
cost per run (usage_ledger) vs. $25 price, refund rate.
